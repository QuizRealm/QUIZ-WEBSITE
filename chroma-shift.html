Welcome to Chroma Shift, one of QuizRealm's premier browser-based puzzle and logic engines. Designed for players who enjoy cerebral challenges, spatial reasoning, and tactical problem-solving, Chroma Shift tests your ability to manipulate interconnected grid networks under strict energy constraints.

How to Play & Pro Tips

Each level presents a 4x4 grid of color-shifting nodes. Tapping a node does two things simultaneously:

  • State Rotation: The clicked tile cycles through its color states (Off → Cyan → Magenta → Off).
  • The Ripple Effect: All direct vertical and horizontal neighbors (up, down, left, right) also shift their states.

Because actions affect neighboring nodes, brute force rarely works on higher levels. Pro Tip: Always work methodically from top-left to bottom-right, using bottom rows to clean up residual misalignments.
