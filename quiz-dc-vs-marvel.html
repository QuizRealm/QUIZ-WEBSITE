The Clash of Titans

Comic book fans have long been divided into two camps: the gods of DC and the marvels of Marvel. This quiz tests your understanding of these storytelling philosophies. DC deals with mythology (Gods among men), while Marvel builds on flawed humanity (outcasts struggling with power).

The Tricky Middle Ground

Batman is DC and Iron Man is Marvel. But do you know where Moon Knight belongs? What about Darkseid vs Thanos? This quiz includes crossovers, copycats, and obscure heroes to test your true allegiance.

Key Differences

  • Setting: DC uses fictional cities (Gotham, Metropolis). Marvel uses real cities (New York).
  • Legacy: DC is big on "legacy heroes" (multiple Robins). Marvel tends to stick to one iconic version.
