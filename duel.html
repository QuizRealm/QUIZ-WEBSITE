A little friendly competition

Pass. Play. Repeat.

Two players, one device, and ten different questions. Choose a topic, take turns, and see who takes the round.

Loading the question bank…

How the same-device duel works

Enter two player names, choose a topic, and start. Player one answers the first question, then passes the device to player two for a different question. You each answer five questions. Every correct answer earns one point, and the higher total wins. Equal totals give a draw.

Questions are hidden behind a handover screen between turns, so you can pass the device without starting the next turn early. By default there is no timer. If you enable the optional 15-second limit, the question clock starts only after the next player presses I’m ready. An unanswered question earns no point when the timer ends.

Make it a good match

Choose mixed knowledge for a broad round or pick a shared interest. A topic you both enjoy usually makes a better contest than one only one player knows. For a larger group on separate phones, try hosting a trivia room.
