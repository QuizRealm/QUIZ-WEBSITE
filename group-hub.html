A simple setup

  1. Open the host screen on a laptop, tablet, or display everyone can see.
  2. Choose a topic and create a room. A four-letter code and QR link will appear.
  3. Players join on their phones and choose a nickname. Use a nickname rather than personal information.
  4. When everyone is in the lobby, start the round. The host controls the pace.

Hosted rooms need an internet connection. If a room cannot connect, try the same-device duel while the service is unavailable.

Playing on one device?

The same-device duel is a simpler option for two players. Take turns, choose an optional timer, and keep the whole game on one screen.
