The Rules of the Grid

Solar Grid is a binary determination puzzle sharing DNA with Sudoku and Picross. The goal is to fill the grid with Light (Sun) and Dark (Moon) without breaking three fundamental laws of logic.

The Three Directives

  • No Triples: You can never have three of the same symbol in a row. If you see two Suns, the next tile must be a Moon.
  • Balance: Every row and column must have an equal number of Suns and Moons.
  • Uniqueness: No two rows or columns can be identical. This is the "fail-safe" rule for hard puzzles.

Cognitive Benefits

Playing Solar Grid engages your prefrontal cortex. Unlike trivia, which relies on recall, this game relies on processing. It creates a flow state that is excellent for anxiety reduction and mental clarity.
