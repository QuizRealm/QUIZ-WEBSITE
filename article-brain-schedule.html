Why Scheduling Matters

Most people play games randomly. They play *Wordle* every day for a year, get really good at 5-letter words, but let their spatial reasoning atrophy.

Neuroplasticity requires variety. By rotating your cognitive load, you force your brain to constantly adapt. Monday stimulates Broca's area (Language). Tuesday fires up the Parietal Lobe (Logic). Wednesday hits the Motor Cortex (Reaction Speed).

This 7-day protocol is designed to prevent "Cognitive Plateau"—where your brain becomes too efficient at one task and stops growing.

Repetition Builds Efficiency (Good)
Variety Builds Resilience (Better)
Intensity Triggers Growth (Best)
