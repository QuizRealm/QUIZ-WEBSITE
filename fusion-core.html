Mastering Fusion Core: Deduction & Logic Strategy

Welcome to Fusion Core, a high-intensity code-breaking and pattern deduction puzzle built for the QuizRealm Arcade. Inspired by classic mastermind logic mechanics, Fusion Core challenges you to uncover a secret color combination using precise diagnostic clues.

How to Play & Pro Tips

Each round generates a hidden sequence of colored tokens. Your goal is to deduce the exact sequence within the allotted attempts:

  • Exact Clues (Green LEDs): Indicates a token of the correct color is placed in the exact correct position.
  • Partial Clues (Yellow LEDs): Indicates a token of the correct color exists in the sequence but is in the wrong position.
  • Escalating Difficulty: Choose between Rookie (3 slots), Agent (4 slots), and Legend (5 slots) to test your cognitive processing speed.
