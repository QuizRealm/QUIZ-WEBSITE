Behind the games

Common Questions

Quick answers about playing, progress, daily challenges, and group rooms.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No. The games are free to open, and the rebuilt player progress works without an account. There are no paid boosts or subscriptions.

Where is my progress saved?

In this browser's local storage. A different browser or device starts with a separate profile. Export a backup from My progress if you want to keep or transfer your save.

When does the Daily Ten reset?

At midnight UTC. Everyone gets the same daily set. A replay does not add another daily completion. Your ordinary play streak counts consecutive local dates with a finished game.

Why did a game not add an achievement?

Achievements have specific requirements shown on the achievements page. Older games may have their own scoring and may not report every activity to the shared profile. Merely opening a page does not count as finishing a game.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Same-device games work on a shared screen. Hosted trivia rooms use the existing Firebase service and an internet connection; other players join with a code or QR link.

Are the reflection quizzes scientific tests?

No. They are recreational prompts with simple scoring and fictional result categories. They cannot diagnose a condition, predict an outcome, or determine compatibility.

How do I report an incorrect answer?

Send the page link and exact question through Contact & corrections. Include a reliable source when possible.

Can I turn off analytics?

Analytics is off unless you choose to allow it. Reopen Privacy choices in the footer to change your preference. Local progress remains available when you choose Essential only.
