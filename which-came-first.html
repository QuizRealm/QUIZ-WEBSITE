Master The Timeline

"Which Came First" is the ultimate test of historical intuition. While some dates are obvious, others reveal the strange non-linearity of human progress. Did you know the Fax Machine was patented (1843) over 30 years before the Telephone (1876)? Or that Nintendo was founded while the Ottoman Empire still existed?

This game challenges your cognitive mapping of history. By forcing you to compare disparate events—like the founding of universities versus the rise of empires—we train your brain to construct a more accurate chronological framework of the world.
