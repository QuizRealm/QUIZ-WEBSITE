Contact & Corrections

Found a broken game, an unclear question, or a problem with the site? Send the details.

The public support address for TheQuizRealm is mustafasharaf2023@gmail.com. This opens your own email application; it does not send a message automatically.

Report a game problem

Please include the page address, what you were trying to do, and what happened instead. The device and browser can also help reproduce a layout or control issue. Do not include passwords, private account information, or personal details about other players.

Suggest a trivia correction

Copy the exact question and the answer choices if possible. Tell us which answer you believe is correct and why. A link to a primary or authoritative source is especially useful for dates, names, and facts that change over time.

Rights and attribution

If you own material used on the site and have a concern, include the exact page or asset URL and enough information to identify the work and your request. The site is independent of the third-party brands and fictional worlds discussed in its games.

Before writing

The common questions page explains local progress, daily resets, and group play. You can export or reset your browser's progress from My progress. There is no subscription or billing account to cancel.
