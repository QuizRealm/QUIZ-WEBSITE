Rapid Fire Trivia

Choose a topic and answer as many questions as you can in sixty seconds. A wrong answer costs five seconds. Every answer gives you something to review.

How Rapid Fire Trivia works

Rapid Fire is a timed quiz about a topic you choose. Start with sixty seconds and answer one multiple-choice question at a time. Correct answers build your score and your current streak; each wrong answer breaks that streak and removes five seconds from the remaining time. The clock keeps running while you switch tabs, so start when you have a minute to spend on the round.

Choose a familiar topic or try something new

The topic menu draws from the site's trivia banks. A round mixes the available difficulty levels for that subject, and the answer positions are shuffled. Read the entire question before choosing: a date, a name, or a word such as “not” can change what the question is asking. If you prefer to think without a timer, choose the untimed quiz link instead.

Use the controls that suit your device

Tap or click an answer, focus a button and press Enter or Space, or use the number keys 1–4 for the corresponding choices. One answer is accepted for each question. Brief feedback shows the correct answer before the next question appears. If you want to stop early, Finish this round saves the answers you have already submitted.

Review the result, rather than only the total

The result shows correct answers out of submitted answers, your longest correct-answer streak, and an expandable review. A question still on screen when the clock expires is not counted as an incorrect answer. An answered round earns 20 completion XP, plus 15 for each correct answer and 10 for each question in your best streak, up to 10,000 XP. A round with no answers earns no XP and adds no completion.

Question sets vary, so scores from different rounds are not identical comparisons. For a slower format, try the Daily Ten or an untimed science quiz. See What a Game Score Means for more about interpreting results.
