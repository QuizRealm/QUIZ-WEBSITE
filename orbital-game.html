Calibrating Your Worldview

We all have a mental map of the world, but how accurate is yours? The World Pulse is a data-driven game designed to expose the gaps in your geographical intuition. It relies on the "Mercator Distortion"—the fact that standard flat maps warp the size of countries near the poles, making Greenland look as big as Africa (spoiler: it's not even close).

The "Higher or Lower" Mechanic

The game presents you with binary choices based on live global statistics. Speed is key, but instinct is your enemy.

  • Population Density: You might know Russia is huge and Bangladesh is small, but do you realize Bangladesh has more people than Russia?
  • Latitude Logic: Which city is further North: Rome, Italy or New York City, USA? (Most people get this wrong because of climate assumptions).
  • Economic Scale: Compare the GDP of California to entire European nations. The scale of the global economy often defies common sense.

Why Play?

In an interconnected world, geographic literacy is a superpower. Understanding the true scale of nations, populations, and economies helps you decode the news and understand global dynamics. This game gamifies the CIA World Factbook, turning dry statistics into a high-stakes test of your mental model of Earth. Adjust your coordinates and jump in.
