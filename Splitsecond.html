Neural Training

The Psychology of Categorization

Split Second trains your brain's semantic memory retrieval under pressure. By forcing you to rapidly group objects, you strengthen cognitive flexibility. The mid-game category shifts force your brain to abandon its current "rule set" and adopt a new one instantly.

Accumulate XP based on your sorting speed and accuracy. Only flawless streaks trigger the 5x multiplier.
