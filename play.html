Seamless Mobile Gameplay

The QuizRealm Controller transforms your smartphone into a dedicated game interface designed for speed and accuracy. Built with "thumb-first" ergonomics, the layout ensures that reaction time is the only barrier between you and victory. Whether you are playing on an iPhone, Android, or tablet, the responsive web design adapts instantly to your screen size without any app store downloads.

Low-latency WebSocket connections via Firebase ensure that your input registers on the host screen in milliseconds. This real-time synchronization is crucial for our speed-based scoring system, where every split-second counts towards your final leaderboard position.

Fair Play & Competitive Scoring

Our proprietary scoring algorithm rewards both knowledge and decisiveness. While a correct answer guarantees points, answering faster than your opponents applies a dynamic multiplier to your score. This prevents ties and keeps the competitive tension high until the final round.

The controller interface provides instant haptic feedback and visual cues to confirm your submission, preventing accidental double-taps. In "Mobile Mode," question text is mirrored directly to your device for accessibility, ensuring that players with a poor view of the main screen are never at a disadvantage.
