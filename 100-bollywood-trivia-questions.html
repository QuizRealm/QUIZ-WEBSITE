Round 1: The 90s Romance & Masala Era

Q01 - Q25

The 1990s redefined the Indian blockbuster. The angry young man of the 70s was replaced by the globalized, hopelessly romantic NRI (Non-Resident Indian). This round tests your knowledge of the Khans, the iconic soundtracks, and the era of oversized suits.

01
In "Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge" (DDLJ), what is the name of the European country where Raj and Simran first meet and fall in love?
Switzerland
02
Which 1994 family drama, starring Salman Khan and Madhuri Dixit, features a dog named Tuffy who essentially acts as a matchmaker?
Hum Aapke Hain Koun..!
03
In "Kuch Kuch Hota Hai", what is the name of the summer camp where Rahul and Anjali reunite after 8 years?
Camp Sunshine
04
Who played the iconic role of the terrifying villain 'Gabar Singh' in the legendary film "Sholay"? (Note: 70s classic, but a mandatory trivia staple).
Amjad Khan
05
In the comedy classic "Andaz Apna Apna", what are the names of the two main characters played by Aamir Khan and Salman Khan?
Amar and Prem
06
Which Shah Rukh Khan movie features the famous dialogue: "Haar kar jeetne wale ko Baazigar kehte hain"?
Baazigar (1993)
07
In "Dil To Pagal Hai", what is the profession of Shah Rukh Khan’s character, Rahul?
Choreographer / Dance Director

The Psychology of Memorizing Cinema

Why is it that we can forget our own bank account passwords, but instantly recall the exact name of the summer camp in Kuch Kuch Hota Hai? In cognitive science, this is attributed to emotional encoding. The brain stores information much more efficiently when it is paired with a strong emotional response or a melodic hook.

The music directors of the 90s—Jatin-Lalit, A.R. Rahman, Nadeem-Shravan—essentially hacked our long-term memory. When you play games in the QuizRealm India Hub, you are not just recalling facts; you are accessing specific neural pathways built by nostalgia.

Round 2: The Modern Era (2000s - 2010s)

Q26 - Q50

The dawn of the 21st century brought urban realism, existential quarter-life crises, and the era of the ensemble cast. Movies stopped being just about grand family weddings and started addressing career burnout, road trips, and modern friendships.

26
In "Zindagi Na Milegi Dobara", what is the specific deep-sea phobia that Hrithik Roshan's character (Arjun) must overcome?
Aquaphobia (Fear of drowning/water)
27
What is the alias used by Rancho (Aamir Khan) in college in the movie "3 Idiots"?
Phunsukh Wangdu
28
In "Yeh Jawaani Hai Deewani", Kabir (Bunny) dreams of working as a videographer for which specific travel network?
The Fox Network (Travel Show)
29
Which 2001 movie, directed by Farhan Akhtar, revolutionized modern Hindi dialogue and focused on three friends in Mumbai?
Dil Chahta Hai
30
In "Jab We Met", Kareena Kapoor's character (Geet) misses her train at which iconic railway station?
Ratlam Junction

Code-Switching and Cinematic Dialogue

A fascinating shift occurred in the early 2000s. Characters stopped speaking in poetic, highly formalized Hindi and Urdu, and began speaking in Hinglish—the exact urban dialect spoken on the streets of Mumbai and Delhi. Round 3: Behind the Scenes & Impossible Lore

Q76 - Q100

This is the cinephile tier. You cannot guess these answers purely by watching the movies. This round requires knowledge of casting decisions, director cameos, and box office anomalies.

76
Who was originally offered the role of 'Tina' in Kuch Kuch Hota Hai before it was eventually accepted by Rani Mukerji?
Twinkle Khanna (The role was written for her)
77
The iconic train sequence at the end of DDLJ was completely improvised. True or False?
False. It was meticulously storyboarded by Aditya Chopra.
78
Which Bollywood movie holds the Guinness World Record for winning the most number of awards for a single film (92 awards)?
Kaho Naa... Pyaar Hai (2000)
79
In "Gangs of Wasseypur", director Anurag Kashyap famously shot the film without what standard piece of equipment to save time?
A clapperboard.
