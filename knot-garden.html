Welcome to The Knot Garden. In a world full of digital noise and high-stress timers, this spatial puzzle is designed to help you slow down, breathe, and engage your lateral logic.

How to Play

  • Drag the Nodes: Use your mouse or finger to tap and drag the circular stones around the garden board.
  • Uncross the Threads: Lines that are crossing each other will glow Terracotta. These are tangled.
  • Achieve Harmony: Position the nodes so that absolutely zero lines cross. Once cleared, the threads will turn Sage Green and the next level will unlock.

The Psychology of Spatial Puzzles

Untangling games operate on a mathematical concept known as Planar Graphs—a graph that can be embedded in the plane so that no edges intersect.

While the math is complex, the human brain is naturally equipped to solve these visual-spatial problems. Playing untangling games activates the parietal lobe, the area of your brain responsible for spatial awareness and navigation. Because there are no timers and no fail states, your brain enters a state of "productive relaxation," making this the perfect mental palate cleanser during a busy workday.
