Mastering European History: The Historian's Decree

Welcome to The Historian's Decree, an immersive historical deduction game built for the QuizRealm Arcade. Test your mastery of European history across critical eras ranging from the Medieval age and Age of Exploration to the Napoleonic and 20th-century conflicts.

How to Play & Strategy

Every scenario presents a historical claim along with three hidden evidence scrolls. Weigh your knowledge carefully:

  • High Risk, High Reward: Answering correctly without opening any scrolls grants the maximum 500 Crown Points.
  • Evidence Scrolls: Flip scrolls to uncover clues, but each clue reduces your maximum payout for that round (350, 200, or 100 points).
  • Royal Decree: Use your one-time Royal Decree power-up to reveal a random scroll for free without lowering your potential score.
