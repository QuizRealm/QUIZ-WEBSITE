About The Intuition Engine

Welcome to The Intuition Engine, a psychological consensus prediction game built for the QuizRealm Arcade. Forget memorizing historical trivia or solving complex math formulas—this game tests your subconscious alignment with global opinion.

How Consensus Scoring Works

Across 20 hand-crafted scenarios, you are presented with ambiguous concepts bounded by two whimsical extremes. Your goal is to slide the meter to the exact percentage where you believe the general population lands:

  • Precision Scoring: Hitting the exact consensus target awards you a maximum 1,000 points per round.
  • Proximity Rewards: Even if you aren't exact, getting within a tight margin yields high scores. Total possible score is 20,000 points.
