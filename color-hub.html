Glassmorphism

2020 - Present

Defined by translucency, multi-layered backgrounds, and subtle borders. It mimics frosted glass using the CSS property backdrop-filter: blur().

The RGB Secret: Low opacity whites (e.g., rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)) layered over saturated gradients.

Neo-Brutalism

The Anti-Design

High contrast, unstyled HTML, stark strokes, and clashing colors. It rejects the "softness" of modern UI.

The RGB Secret: Pure, unadulterated primary colors. #FF0000, #0000FF, #FFFF00. No gradients. No alpha channels.

Acid Graphics

Chrome & Liquid

Inspired by late 90s rave flyers. Chrome type, liquid metal distortion, and neon colors against dark backgrounds.

The RGB Secret: Maxing out saturation. Combining colours at the opposite ends of the spectrum (e.g., Neon Green + Hot Pink) to cause "visual vibration."
