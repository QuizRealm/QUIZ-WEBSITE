About Rank It: The Subjective Tier List Game

Welcome to Rank It, an interactive tier-list challenge built for the QuizRealm Arcade. This game strips away standard trivia and forces you to rank hilariously relatable, chaotic, and everyday human experiences from best to worst.

How to Play & Strategy

Every round presents 5 randomized items centered around a specific theme or dilemma. Using touch or mouse controls, drag and drop the items into your preferred order:

  • Position #1: Reserved for your ultimate champion or coping mechanism.
  • Position #5: Your absolute dealbreaker or minor inconvenience.
  • The Roast Engine: Once you lock in your choices, our diagnostic engine evaluates your top and bottom picks and delivers a witty psychological judgment.
