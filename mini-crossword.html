The Sprint of Word Puzzles

The traditional Sunday crossword is a marathon—a slow, coffee-sipping experience that takes hours. Vortex Cross is a sprint. It’s a 5x5 grid designed to be solved in under two minutes. But don't let the small size fool you; the density of the clues means every single letter counts. There are no "filler" words here.

Anatomy of a 5x5 Grid

In a standard 15x15 puzzle, you have room to guess. In a Vortex Cross, the grid is tight. Getting 1-Across wrong can ripple down and destroy your chances for 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5-Down instantly. This requires a different strategy:

  • Scan for Anchors: Don't solve in order. Find the one clue you are 100% sure of (usually a proper noun or pop culture reference) and use that as your anchor.
  • Mind the Vowels: In a 5x5 grid, vowels are the structural beams. If you can place the 'A's and 'E's, the rest of the grid often reveals itself.
  • The "Cross" Check: Never write a word in pen (or digital ink) until you've verified at least one crossing clue. The ambiguity of short words (e.g., "ATE" vs "EAT") is the most common trap.

A short word puzzle

Across and down clues intersect, so filling one answer can give letters to another. Start with a clue you recognise, then revisit the unsolved entries. A single completed grid is a useful stopping point when you only have a few minutes.
