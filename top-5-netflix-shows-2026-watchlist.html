03
Comfort Comedy Workplace Satire Perpetual Loop

The Office (The Eternal Rewatch)

Why We're Obsessed: Cognitive Decompression

Yes, it ended years ago. Yes, you have already seen every episode five times. Yet, The Office consistently ranks in the top 5 most-streamed shows globally. Why do we keep going back to Dunder Mifflin when there is an infinite library of new, high-budget content available?

Psychologists refer to this as "Cognitive Decompression." When your daily life is unpredictable and stressful, your brain seeks out media where the outcome is entirely known. It requires zero processing power to watch Michael Scott burn his foot on a George Foreman grill. It is safe, familiar, and restorative. If you rely heavily on this show to unwind, you might be experiencing Workplace Burnout and using the show to self-medicate.
