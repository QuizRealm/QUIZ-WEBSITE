How to play Memory Trail

Watch the numbered tiles light up, then tap them in the same order. Each successful round adds one tile. A mistake ends the run. The game stops after twelve completed rounds. You can use a mouse, touch, or Tab and Enter. Focus on a small group of positions at a time rather than trying to name the whole sequence.

A good challenge, without a verdict

This is a recreational browser game. Your score describes this run on this device. It is not a measure of intelligence, health, attention, or any medical condition. Play at a comfortable pace, and take a break whenever you want.

Keep your small wins

A completed run is added to your local game history and earns a small amount of XP. Visit your profile to see your progress, or explore more word and logic games for a different kind of challenge.
